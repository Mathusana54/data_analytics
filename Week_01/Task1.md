1. Problem definition (identify one or more potential problem(s)).
    Problem: People looking for an apartment in the Canton of Zurich find it difficult to assess whether an apartment’s rent is reasonable because the price depends on many factors at the same time.

    Background:

    - A housing shortage and high demand are driving rents up.
    - Location: Apartments in the city are more expensive, while those in rural communities are cheaper.
    - Condition and amenities: Newly built apartments are more expensive than older ones.
   -  Accessibility: Proximity to public transportation or shopping areas affects the value.
    - These factors interact with one another, which is why it’s difficult to directly compare listings.

2. Objective (define a clear objective based on the problem definition).
    Develop a model taht estimates the rent for an apartment in the Canton of Zurich based on its characteristics and those of its municipality.

3. Research question (formulate a research question based on the objective).
    What factors (housing characteristics and community characteristics) influence rental prices for apartments in the Canton of Zurich and how well can these factors be used to.

4. Hypothesis (formulate a clear hypothesis based on the research question).
    Living space, the number of rooms, and location (e.g., population desity or the municipality's tax rate) have a significant impact on rent. Aparments in municipalities with higher population densities are more expensive for the same amount of living space.

5. Expected added value of the data analysis (business value of data analytics).
    - People looking for an apartment can spot overpriced listings and better compare offers.
    - Landlord can set a price in line with market (fewer vacancies, no underpricing).
    - Real estate platforms can offer a price estimate as an additional feature.
